- Music casting through a phone walking out of the room or between mesh Wi-Fi nodes, improved again: heartbeats and audio stopped one after the other and the TV added the two silences together, ending the cast after only a few seconds without audio. The TV now counts from the last sign of life of the phone (heartbeat or audio) and takes it for gone only after 30 quiet seconds; if it comes back in that time the music carries on.

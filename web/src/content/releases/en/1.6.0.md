- Casting verification now covers apps' own cast button (DLNA) too: apps can't enter a PIN or password, so under Confirm, PIN or Password the TV asks the first time a phone casts from an app. Such phones are listed under "Devices that have cast" as the app and the phone's network address, and can be blocked there.
- A blocked device, or one not yet allowed, can no longer pause, stop, seek or change the volume over DLNA.
- "When another device casts" now applies across AirPlay and DLNA both ways: with Refuse it (the default), an iPhone no longer replaces a DLNA video that is playing, and a second phone can't take over another's DLNA cast. Stop it on the TV first, or choose Let it take over.
- Fixed a possible crash on Android 6 when screen mirroring or music ended.

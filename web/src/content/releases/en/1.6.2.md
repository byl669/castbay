- Fixed a crash when an app cast a DASH video (.mpd) over DLNA: such videos now play, and a format the TV can't play shows a message instead of closing CastBay.
- HLS and DASH videos from apps play even when their link doesn't end in .m3u8 or .mpd, as long as the app says which they are.
- FLAC music is offered to apps only on Android 8.1 and later, the first versions that can play it.
- The compatibility page lists the formats CastBay plays over DLNA.

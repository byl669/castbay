- More reliable start: if local network discovery never gets ready, the receiver no longer stays on "Starting…" for ever. After 30 seconds it shows an error with Retry, and after an error it starts again by itself, up to three times, which helps a TV or a car nobody is watching.
- Some TV boxes (an Android 9 one, for example) fail to register the receiver with the system's service ("Local network discovery failed"), so phones cannot find the TV: when the system's service will not register, CastBay now announces itself on the network, and phones can still find it.

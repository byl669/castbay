- Series cast over DLNA go straight on to the next episode: when iQiyi moves on (at the closing credits, or when you pick the next episode), the TV stays on the video and plays the next one, instead of flashing CastBay's home screen in between.
- A DLNA video that plays to its end shows "Waiting for the next video…" for up to 30 seconds, so the app can cast the next one; press Back to stop.
- Subtitle files sent with a DLNA video (SRT, WebVTT, ASS/SSA, TTML; from NAS and media-server apps, for example) are shown, Chinese files in GBK included, and can be turned off in the quick menu.

- New "Settings → Playback → Open When Casting" (off by default): when a device starts mirroring or casting a video while CastBay is not on screen (the TV on its home screen or in another app), CastBay now opens by itself, so the picture shows instead of a blank TV. Android needs "Display over other apps" for this: turning the switch on opens that settings page. Without it, the TV only shows a note to open CastBay. Music does not open the screen.

- Fixed the receiver taking several seconds to come up when the app is opened again right after quitting on some devices: opened again soon after quitting, the app now starts the receiver itself and it is ready in about 1 to 3 seconds (the system could drop the request to start the service, which meant 5 to 15 seconds of waiting).
- New "Settings → General → Receive Test Versions" (off by default): with it on, a test version (marked beta) is offered in About when one is published, to download and install directly and help check a fix; the release that follows is offered after it. Test versions may be less stable.
